MOVEUP SCREEN TIME

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how TWORAPPS handles information in connection with MOVEUP Screen Time. The precise behavior of the published app must be checked against its final release before this policy is used for App Store submission.

Information and permissions

MOVEUP may request access to Apple's Screen Time-related APIs to let you select apps, apply limits and manage exercise-based rewards. MOVEUP may request camera permission to verify movements during supported exercises. These permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.

Exercise and app settings

MOVEUP may store settings, exercise progress, reward history and parent PIN-related configuration on your device to operate its features. The exact storage and synchronization behavior depends on the released app version.

Camera

Camera access is intended for movement verification, not for recording or sharing videos. Before release, TWORAPPS will confirm whether any images, video frames or derived movement data leave the device and update this policy accordingly.

Subscriptions

If you purchase MOVEUP Pro, purchases and subscription billing are handled through Apple's App Store. Apple may process transaction information according to its own privacy policies. MOVEUP may read subscription status to unlock purchased features.

Data sharing and analytics

Any third-party analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage or other data sharing introduced in the released app must be disclosed here and in App Store Connect before publication.

Children and parental controls

Parents or guardians should supervise setup where appropriate. Do not share parent PINs with support. MOVEUP is not a substitute for parental supervision.

Your choices

You can manage camera and Screen Time permissions in iOS Settings and manage subscriptions through your Apple Account. For privacy questions or requests, contact rampante07@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as MOVEUP develops. The date above will reflect the latest revision.
