Frequently asked questions

Why does MOVEUP request Screen Time permission?

MOVEUP uses Apple's Screen Time-related permissions to manage selected apps and support exercise-based screen time rewards. You can review or revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

How do I select an app?

Open MOVEUP, grant the requested permission and use the app selection screen to choose which apps to manage. Available options depend on your MOVEUP plan.

Why isn't an app being blocked?

Check Screen Time authorization, selected apps and configured limits. If the issue continues, restart MOVEUP and contact support with your iOS version.

Why does MOVEUP need the camera?

The camera is used for movement verification during supported exercises. Grant camera access when prompted. If access was denied, review it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Select MOVEUP to view or cancel a subscription. Removing the app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

How does the seven-day Pro access work?

MOVEUP is designed to offer new users seven days of Pro access without payment details. After that, users can continue with the Free features or choose a paid subscription. Availability is subject to the version released.

What if I forget the parent PIN?

Use the recovery option provided within MOVEUP, if available in your installed version. For help, contact support. Never send your PIN by email.
